I am an AV engineer who installs and troubleshoots systems for a living. I also hold four Oracle Cloud certifications and just passed my CTS from AVIXA. On paper, that sounds like a person who has it figured out.

I don't.

What I have is a clear direction, a lot of ground to cover, and a decision to document every step of it honestly — the wins, the confusion, and the hard lessons in between.

<!-- truncate -->

## Why this site exists

I work in post production and support at an AV integration company in Lagos. My days involve rack building, support tickets, preventive maintenance, and documentation. It is hands-on, practical work and I genuinely enjoy it.

But I know where I want to go — cloud infrastructure, security, and building systems that sit at the intersection of physical and digital environments. Getting there requires deliberate effort and I have decided that documenting that effort publicly is the most honest way to hold myself accountable.

## What you will find here

This blog will cover:

- Things I am learning — certifications, tools, concepts
- Projects I am building — starting with an AI-powered knowledge management system for AV engineers
- Honest reflections on the process of possibly transitioning careers/ pivoting while working full time

No fluff. No performance. Just the work.

## A note on the name

Prof Gabs is a nickname that stuck. I am not a professor. But I have always believed that the best way to learn something is to teach it — to document it clearly enough that someone else can follow. That is what this site is.

Welcome to my journey.
